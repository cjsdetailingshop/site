SMALL SHOP.
SERIOUS STANDARDS.

CJ's Detailing and Polishing Shop was built around a simple idea: take pride in the work, treat every vehicle with care, and never rush through the details.

What started with detailing vehicles for people in the neighborhood has grown into an independent, appointment-based drop-off detailing business in Turlock. I focus on giving each vehicle the time and attention it deserves, whether it needs an interior reset, a clean exterior, or paint polishing.

Thanks for considering my shop. I look forward to helping you bring your vehicle back to its best.
