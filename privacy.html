Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how CJ's Detailing and Polishing Shop handles information submitted through this website.

Information you choose to provide

If you use the quote form, your name, phone number, vehicle type, requested service, and details you enter are placed into a text message addressed to the shop. The website does not send that message automatically. You choose whether to send it from your own messaging app.

How information is used

Information you send may be used to respond to quote requests, discuss services, schedule appointments, and communicate about your vehicle. It is not sold by this website.

Website hosting and technical data

The website host may collect standard technical information such as browser type, device information, and server logs. The exact data collected depends on the hosting provider used when the site is published.

Third-party services

Links to email, text messaging, and Instagram open third-party services. Their own privacy policies and practices apply when you use those services.

Data requests

To ask about information you have sent to the shop or request that it be corrected or deleted where appropriate, contact cavj.newell@gmail.com.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated if website features or business practices change. The revision date will be changed when updates are made.
